INTEGRATIONS · PER WORKSPACE

Connections

Connect any CRM with a compatible REST endpoint, or bridge a platform with webhooks.

⌑ Workspace-scoped settings
▤

CRM REST adapter

Send new lead records as JSON to an endpoint that accepts POST. Use n8n-compatible public HTTPS webhooks to map fields to your CRM.

ⓘIntegration URLs must use public HTTPS on port 443. Private or local network destinations and redirects are blocked.
⤳

Messaging and scheduling

Public HTTPS webhook receivers can bridge to SMS, calendar, automation, and voice providers.

✳

Public LLM provider

Connect a supported cloud model for lead qualification and CRM-context questions.

ⓘAPI keys are encrypted per workspace. Lead details sent to chat or qualification are shared with the chosen provider under its data policies. Choose the provider and model you want; local rules are used for qualification when no LLM is configured.
